Problem Statement

Why Binary "Fake News" Labels Fail Readers

Modern persuasion campaigns blend half-truths, selective framing, and synthetic imagery. Telling a reader "82% Fake" creates defensiveness rather than media literacy — and naively flagging keywords produces high false positives on journalism and encyclopedias.

Traditional Detectors
  • Single black-box score with zero pedagogical explanation.
  • Keyword triggers flag educational pages discussing propaganda ("Hard Negatives").
  • Ignore embedded visuals and synthetic AI-generated imagery.
  • Single-model bias goes unchecked without voting consensus.
The CheckEm Approach
  • Teaches the "Why": Names the exact technique and quotes the trigger phrase.
  • Separates Tone from Trust: Evaluates who says it (Provenance) separately from how they frame it (Rhetoric).
  • Multimodal VLM Jury: 3 independent VLMs vote per page, per section, and per image.
  • Hard-Negative Calibrated: Proven 0% FPR on Wikipedia articles defining propaganda.

Pillar 1 — Name the Technique

Pedagogical Taxonomy Across Text & Visuals

Every flagged section or image highlights the trigger quote and attaches a plain-language explanation so readers learn to spot the tactic independently next time.

Loaded Language

Emotionally charged epithets pushing outrage or moral condemnation before facts are examined.

Fear Appeal

Escalates existential dread or imminent catastrophe to bypass deliberative judgment.

Whataboutism

Deflects accountability on a claim by pointing to an unrelated fault of critics.

Fabricated Authority

Invokes unverifiable institutes, secret studies, or anonymous insiders for unearned credibility.

False Dilemma

Collapses complex policy trade-offs into two extreme binary choices.

Scapegoating

Blames a broad group or attacks speaker motives rather than addressing evidence.

Synthetic Image

Detects generative artifacts (texture smoothness, lighting anomalies, warped geometry) via vision pixels.

Visual Framing

Identifies staged or decontextualized visuals paired with sensationalist captions.

Pillar 2 — Trust the Source

Four-Signal Page Provenance Scoring (0–100)

Even persuasive advocacy from a verifiable organization (eff.org, Trust 87) differs fundamentally from an unverified synthetic outlet (civic-pulse-daily, Trust 32). CheckEm surfaces all four provenance sub-scores explicitly:

1. Domain & Publisher Identity (32%)

Checks HTTPS security, TLD authority (.gov, .int, .org), subdomain anomalies, and established editorial registry heuristics.

2. Content Originality (20%)

Measures unique structural block ratio vs duplicated/cloned boilerplate across extracted sections and depth of original prose.

3. Human & Visual Authenticity (24%)

Combines sentence-length burstiness/variance statistics with VLM image synthesis flags and fabricated-source penalties.

4. Citation & Link Network Integrity (24%)

Counts outbound reference links and distinct cited domains vs unlinked assertions citing "suppressed reports".

Subchallenge — Open-Weights Gemma VL

Going Beyond Zero-Shot with Fine-Tuned Gemma

Because Gemma is an open-weights VLM, we can tune and inspect it directly rather than treating it as an opaque API (see challenges/gemma/README.md).

1. QLoRA Span Adaptation

4-bit NF4 LoRA fine-tuning (r=16, alpha=32) on SemEval-2020 Task 11 propaganda techniques + encyclopedic hard negatives for tight span boundaries.

2. Logprob Thresholding

Access token log-probabilities for "questionable" vs "clean" to tune precision vs recall explicitly and suppress borderline false positives.

3. Pedagogical Counterfactuals

Adapter also teaches Gemma to generate a neutral rewrite beside flagged quotes, showing readers how loaded adjectives shift tone.

Subchallenge — Nemotron 3.5 VL & Verda Cloud GPUs

Logical Fallacy Jury + Self-Hosted GPU Inference

Nemotron 3.5 VL serves as our reasoning-focused fallacy & visual integrity juror, hosted on Verda Cloud GPU instances via OpenAI-compatible vLLM (see challenges/nemotron/ & challenges/verda-cloud/).

Nemotron 3.5 VL Specialization

  • Employs structured reasoning traces to test implication links (critical for Whataboutism & False Dilemma).
  • Discriminates describing a fallacy vs using a fallacy to persuade.
  • Pairs vision inspection with chart/meme OCR context analysis.
  • Custom reasoning-distilled LoRA training supported via challenges/nemotron/.

Verda Cloud GPU Cluster Deployment

  • Provision an A100/H100 VM on Verda Cloud with Docker + NVIDIA Container Toolkit.
  • Run deploy_verda.sh to serve both fine-tuned Gemma VL & Nemotron 3.5 VL LoRA adapters over vLLM.
  • Point VERDA_GPU_BASE_URL in .env; backend routes Gemma & Nemotron straight to Verda with zero downtime fallback.
